Most agencies open with a 90-slide "AI transformation roadmap" and a six-figure retainer. Ours opens with a pen, a whiteboard, and your own people.

Tokens are our cost, not yours. Seats are a rounding error. You pay a rate per finished unit — a triaged claim, a tagged lease, a resolved ticket, an answered call — with an SLA in the contract. Miss the SLA, you don't pay.
