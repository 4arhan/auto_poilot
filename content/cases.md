## From a workflow audit to a *billed outcome*.

Every engagement follows the same four-beat. Two weeks in your team, two weeks in build, then agents running against a contract SLA — billed per finished unit, credited back when they miss.

01

#### Workflow audit

Ten working days observing the job end-to-end. Maps the manual path, finds the bottleneck, prices only what we can ship.

02

#### Agent build

Wire the agent into your stack — EMR, ERP, CRM, phone — with audit logs, credit-back SLA, and a read-only ops console for your team.

03

#### Shadow + cutover

Agent runs alongside humans for a sign-off window. Confidence scores reviewed, threshold agreed, cutover signed by a named owner.

04

#### In production

Every outcome timestamped, billed per finished unit, audited on the console. Miss SLA, the outcome is free — automatic on the invoice.
