Section IV · The fine print
04

Frequently asked

## Questions we *want* you to ask.

Ten answers to the questions every CFO asks before they'll sign a variable-cost line. If your question isn't here, email us — we publish new answers monthly.

01What exactly is an "outcome"?+

An outcome is a finished unit of work, defined in the statement of work before we start. For a discovery agent, it's a document tagged and written to Relativity. For a reconciliation agent, it's a ledger entry drafted and approved. *Every outcome has a pass/fail SLA criterion* — we don't bill the fails.

02What happens if an agent misses the SLA?+

The outcome doesn't count. It's automatically credited back on your next invoice — no ticket, no dispute. Our SLA also includes a cumulative clause: *if we miss 2% or more in a calendar month,* the whole month's run is credited back, not just the misses.

03Do you pass on inference / model costs?+

No. Token cost is our problem, not yours. If models get cheaper, we keep the margin — and lower our published rate card at the next quarterly review. We publish the rate-card change history so you can hold us to it.

04Currency? We're not in Australia.+

Default is AUD. We publish USD and GBP rate cards for clients domiciled there — same underlying rates, converted monthly at OANDA mid. We invoice in your base currency so you don't eat FX volatility.

05Minimum term & exit terms?+

30-day rolling after the first 90 days. You can churn in month four, no notice period, no exit fee. *On churn we hand over agent weights, logs, and export artefacts within 30 days* — and purge our side. That's in the contract, not a goodwill policy.

06How do you handle regulated data?+

We're ISO 27001, SOC 2 Type II, HIPAA-aligned and Privacy-Act-compliant. Data residency is contracted per-region: AU, EU, UK, US, SG. We never train shared models on your corpus — every vertical agent is a per-tenant fine-tune you own.

07What's the minimum volume?+

100 outcomes / month for outcome pricing. Below that, the engineering cost doesn't clear — we'll tell you so during the audit and point you at better-suited tools.

08Do you charge for the audit if we don't proceed?+

No. Audits are free whether you hire us, hire someone else, or do nothing. We do cap audits at 18 / quarter — so they're first-come, first-served.

09Can you build a bespoke agent outside the catalogue?+

If it's inside our thirteen verticals, usually yes. Bespoke agents carry a one-time build fee (A$18k–A$48k) *plus* the same per-outcome rate card. Outside the thirteen verticals, we'll politely decline. Gulf engagements (UAE, SA) priced in AED or SAR.

10Who owns the IP?+

You own: the agent weights trained on your data, the logs, and anything the agent produces. We own: the base framework and the tenant-agnostic tooling. On exit, your agent runs on your infra without us — we leave you with a container, a README, and a phone number.
